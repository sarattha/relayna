# Relayna ecosystem

Relayna connects durable task execution, governed access, sandbox isolation, and operational visibility for agent applications.

1. [Run the local quickstart](quickstart.md) to submit a task, replay its events, and open its result in Studio.
2. [Read the architecture](architecture.md) to see who owns the task state, execution policy, audit evidence, and usage.
3. [Choose a deployment profile](deployment-profiles.md) based on your access, observability, and isolation needs.

The [component directory](components.md) links the standalone repositories and their release channels. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) describes the integration milestones and the remaining production work.
